Revoking a client is permanent by design — a revoked certificate can never be reactivated, only replaced. Two separate actions are available here, and they do very different things: Restore issues a brand-new certificate under the same name (a fresh identity that happens to share a name, not the old one coming back), while Permanently Delete only clears out the old certificate's leftover files on disk — it never removes the revocation record itself, which stays forever for audit history.
