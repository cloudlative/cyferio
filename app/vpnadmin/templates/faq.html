Getting Started

What is a VPN, and why am I being asked to use one?

A VPN (Virtual Private Network) creates a private, encrypted connection between your device and this organization's network -- like a locked tunnel that keeps what you send and receive hidden from anyone else on the same Wi-Fi or internet connection. It's what lets you securely reach internal systems from home, a coffee shop, or while traveling, the same way you could if you were sitting in the office.

How do I log in to {{ app_settings.app_name }}?

Go to the login page and enter the username and password your administrator gave you. If this is your very first time logging in, you'll likely be asked to set a new password right away -- that's expected, not an error.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Click Forgot password? on the login page and enter the email address on your account. If we find a match, we'll send you a link to reset your password -- it works once and expires after a while, so use it soon after it arrives. If you don't have an email on file, or the email never arrives, contact your administrator and ask them to reset it for you instead.

Why does it say "you must reset your password" when I log in?

This shows up right after your account is created, or any time an administrator resets your password for you. It's a safety check to make sure the temporary password only you and the administrator briefly knew gets replaced with one only you know. Just follow the on-screen form to choose a new password -- you won't be able to use the rest of the site until you do.

Connecting to the VPN

Where do I get my VPN profile / connection file?

Open My VPN Profile from the menu, then use the Download .ovpn button. This downloads a single file (ending in .ovpn) that contains everything an OpenVPN app needs to connect as you -- think of it like a personalized key. Keep it somewhere private and don't share it; anyone with the file could connect using your identity.

How do I actually connect once I have the file?

Step 1: Get the OpenVPN Connect app. It's free, and you only need to install it once.

Download OpenVPN Connect ↗

Step 2: Import your profile. Open the app, choose "Import Profile" or "Add" and select the .ovpn file you downloaded, then tap Connect. You'll only need to import the file once -- after that, connecting is a single tap.

What's a "MAC address" and why is it asking me to add mine?

Every device (phone, laptop, tablet) has a unique hardware ID called a MAC address -- think of it as a serial number for its network hardware. For security, your VPN profile only lets in devices whose MAC address has been added to your allowed list first, from My VPN Profile. If you're connecting from a new device and it won't let you in, this is usually why -- add that device's MAC address there.

You can usually find a device's MAC address under its Wi-Fi or network settings, often labeled "Wi-Fi Address" or "Wi-Fi MAC Address".

I can't connect, or my connection keeps dropping. What should I check?

A few common causes, roughly in order of how often they're the actual problem:

1. The device you're using hasn't been added under My VPN Profile → Allowed Devices yet (see the MAC address question above).
2. Your monthly bandwidth allowance has run out -- check My VPN Profile for your current usage.
3. Your account was revoked or is temporarily deactivated -- ask your administrator to check its status.
4. The network you're on is blocking VPN traffic (some public/hotel Wi-Fi does this) -- try a different network if possible.

If none of these explain it, see "Still need help?" below.

My Account & Usage

How do I change my password?

Go to My Profile (click your name in the top-right corner), where you'll find a form to set a new password. You'll need to enter your current password first.

What does "bandwidth quota" mean, and what happens if I use it all?

Some accounts have a monthly data allowance (measured in GB) for how much they can send and receive over the VPN. You can see your current usage and remaining allowance under My VPN Profile. If you run out before the month ends, your connection may be limited until it resets on the 1st -- if that happens and it's a problem, ask your administrator about increasing your allowance. Accounts with no quota set have unlimited usage.

Is my activity on the VPN private?

The VPN encrypts your connection so it can't be read by others on the same network, but your organization's administrators can see basic connection details for accounts they manage (when you connected, how much data you used, which device) as part of normal network administration -- the same way any workplace network can see this. If you have questions about your organization's specific privacy policy, ask your administrator.

I got a new phone or laptop -- what do I need to do?

Two steps: add the new device's MAC address under My VPN Profile → Allowed Devices, then install the OpenVPN Connect app on it and import your .ovpn file (you can download it again from the same page any time -- it doesn't expire after one use). You don't need a new profile for a new device, the same one works everywhere you've added.
